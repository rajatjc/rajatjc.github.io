CareerLinkup | Empowering Connections in the Job Market

CareerLinkup is a comprehensive career services platform that fosters connections between job seekers and employers, revolutionizing the job search process.

As a key contributor to the project, I took charge of multiple facets:

  • Enabling job seekers to build personalized portfolios through seamless user registration, login, and profile creation.
  • Equipping employers with streamlined control over company profiles, enhancing their ability to connect with potential candidates.
  • Designing an intuitive job posting and browsing system, which empowers employers to create detailed job listings and job seekers to find and apply for suitable positions.
  • Elevating the platform's analytical capabilities by revamping analytics and reporting features. This enhancement provides employers with valuable insights into application metrics, job post performance, and user engagement, facilitating data-driven decision-making.
